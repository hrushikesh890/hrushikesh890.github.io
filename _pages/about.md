I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering at [North Carolina State University](https://www.ncsu.edu/), specializing in quantum computing. My research focuses on quantum error mitigation, quantum error correction and characterization, and quantum machine learning, with the broader goal of making near-term and early fault-tolerant quantum computation more reliable.

I hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Stony Brook University, where I worked on variational quantum linear solvers, and a B.E. in Electronics and Telecommunications from the Pune Institute of Computer Technology, Pune, India.

My work combines high-performance numerical simulation, GPU-accelerated computing, and object-oriented software development for hybrid quantum&ndash;classical workflows. I've had the opportunity to intern at [SRI](https://www.sri.com/) working on quantum reinforcement learning benchmarks, and as a Fellowship Student at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://www.lanl.gov/), where I worked on QAOA-based warm-start optimizations for the Quantum Linear System Problem.

Outside of research, I enjoy mentoring high school and undergraduate students, reading science fiction and fantasy, and traveling.
