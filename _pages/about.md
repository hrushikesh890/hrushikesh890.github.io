I am a Postdoctoral Research Scholar in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.ncsu.edu/) at [North Carolina State University](https://www.ncsu.edu/), working in the quantum information science group of [Dr. Sabre Kais](https://ece.ncsu.edu/people/skais/). I work on fault-tolerant quantum computing, especially quantum error correction, magic state preparation and decoding, along with error mitigation, quantum machine learning, and the simulation of open and non-equilibrium quantum systems.

My current research interests include:

- **Fault-tolerant quantum computing:** magic state cultivation, lattice surgery, and resource estimation
- **Quantum error correction:** decoding, code and circuit design, and characterization
- **Quantum error mitigation**
- **Quantum machine learning**
- **Open quantum systems** and non-equilibrium quantum dynamics

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from NC State in May 2026, advised by Dr. Huiyang Zhou, with a dissertation titled [_Reliable Quantum Computation From NISQ to FTQC Era_](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&user=ZJe4U4AAAAAJ&citation_for_view=ZJe4U4AAAAAJ:eQOLeE2rZwMC). I also hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Stony Brook University, where I worked on variational quantum linear solvers, and a B.E. in Electronics and Telecommunications from the Pune Institute of Computer Technology, Pune, India.

My work combines high-performance numerical simulation, GPU-accelerated computing, and object-oriented software development for hybrid quantum&ndash;classical workflows. I've had the opportunity to intern at [SRI](https://www.sri.com/) working on quantum reinforcement learning benchmarks, and as a Fellowship Student at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://www.lanl.gov/), where I worked on QAOA-based warm-start optimizations for the Quantum Linear System Problem.

Outside of research, I enjoy mentoring high school and undergraduate students, reading science fiction and fantasy, and traveling.
